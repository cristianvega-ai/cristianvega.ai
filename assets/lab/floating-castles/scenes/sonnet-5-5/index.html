Skyhold

Everything you see is generated by code when the page loads — no models, no textures, no downloads. A castle, a forest, a valley, clouds and birds, all built from voxels and lit by hand-written shaders.

  • World · noise-carved rock island, rivers carved to the rim, voxel castle, village, ~1,000 trees, a lake, a lighthouse, a valley with pools where each waterfall lands
  • Voxel renderer · custom mesher with vertex ambient occlusion, baked sky visibility, wind-swayed foliage and banners
  • Lighting · soft PCSS sun shadows, moving cloud shadows, height-fog aerial perspective, a full day–night cycle
  • Water · flowing moat and rivers, ballistic waterfalls that dissolve into mist, spray and plunge pools
  • Post · HDR, MSAA, bloom, god rays, filmic grading
  • Life · boids flocks and eagles, butterflies, fireflies, petals, smoke, lanterns, boats, a turning windmill, procedural sound

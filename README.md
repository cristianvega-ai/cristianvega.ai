# cristianvega.ai

[cristianvega.ai](https://cristianvega.ai) is Cristian Vega's personal website.
It presents his work in production AI, engineering leadership, and agentic systems.

## Website sections

| Section | What you can find |
| --- | --- |
| Home | An introduction to Cristian and links to his latest work. |
| About | His professional background, experience, and approach to building AI systems. |
| Writing | Notes and essays about production AI, engineering, and the systems around them. |
| Products | Products he has built, with descriptions and links when available. |

Writing appears after an article is published.
The Products section appears after a product is published.
Until then, the homepage marks each section as coming soon.
